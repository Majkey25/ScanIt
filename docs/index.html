Common questions.

Does ScanIt upload my documents?

No scanned document content is sent to a maintainer-operated server. Google says ML Kit processes scanner input on-device. ML Kit does process limited diagnostic and usage telemetry; the details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why does it use Google Play services?

Google Play services supplies the document scanner UI, models, automatic capture, crop, and filters. The module may need to download or update before first use.

Are Recent scans permanent?

No. They are bounded temporary working copies and Android may clear them. Removing one from Recent removes that working copy. If ScanIt can verify the exact saved outputs it created, the confirmation dialog can also delete the selected PDF, Gallery images, or both.

Does uninstalling ScanIt remove saved files?

Uninstall clears ScanIt's settings and private cache. It does not delete PDFs or images already saved in shared storage or a folder you selected.

Is the public repository open source?

No. Current code is source-visible under proprietary terms. Unmodified official binaries may be installed and used. Historical copies already released under MIT keep their original MIT grant.
