Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 2026-08-24

This policy describes the public ScanIt Android app and this ScanIt website. Contact the maintainer at majkeylab@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not email private documents or credentials.

Short version: scanning and document processing stay on-device. Stable ScanIt has no accounts, ads, first-party analytics, maintainer-operated document server, or generative-AI client. The ScanIt VIP Ads prerelease contains Google advertising, consent, and Play Billing SDKs but does not send document content to them.

Information the app handles locally

ScanIt handles pages you scan or import, the generated PDF, local saving preferences, language choice, selected folder access, and email subject or message defaults. This information is used only to provide the requested scanning, saving, sharing, printing, and local cleanup functions.

Temporary working copies

Pages and PDFs are copied to ScanIt's app-private cache. The cache is bounded to at most eight scan directories and supplies the Result and Recent scans screens. Android may clear it. Removing an item from Recent removes that temporary working copy. When ScanIt can verify an exact saved output that it created, the confirmation dialog can also delete the selected PDF, Gallery images, or both.

Durable saved files

According to your settings and actions, ScanIt can save images to Gallery or a folder selected through Android's folder picker and PDFs to Downloads or a selected folder. They remain until you delete them through ScanIt, Gallery, Files, or the selected-folder provider. Optional settings can delete the matching saved PDF or images after you choose a sharing app. PDF deletion is enabled on a fresh install and image deletion is disabled; both settings can be changed. Uninstalling ScanIt does not remove saved files outside app-private storage.

Settings, backup, and deletion

Settings are stored locally in app-private storage. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for ScanIt app data. Uninstalling the app removes its private settings and cache. It does not remove files saved outside the app-private area.

Google ML Kit Document Scanner

The scanner UI, models, and related resources are supplied through Google Play services. Google says document-scanner input and output are processed on-device and are not sent to Google servers. Google Play services may contact Google to download or update the scanner module, models, bug fixes, or compatibility information.

Optional text extraction and QR/barcode detection use ML Kit recognition models delivered by Google Play services and run on-device. A model may download before first use. ScanIt does not upload document content or detected results to a maintainer-operated service. Extracted text is written only to a document destination you select, and copied recognition results are placed on Android's clipboard with the sensitive-content flag. Google states that ML Kit collects device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output size, feature version, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this telemetry is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties. ScanIt cannot disable or delete telemetry processed by Google Play services.

See Google's current ML Kit Terms and Privacy information, Android data disclosure, and Privacy Policy.

Local document cleanup

Smart cleanup and Manual cleanup process document pixels on-device. Manual cleanup reads only a bounded area around each user-drawn loop and creates a reversible child revision; the parent remains in the bounded Recent cache. ScanIt does not send cleanup input or output to a cloud AI service.

Sharing and printing

ScanIt sends a selected PDF or image to another app or print service only after you tap a share or print action and choose a recipient. That recipient processes the file under its own privacy terms. Review the destination before sending sensitive material.

ScanIt VIP Ads prerelease

The VIP Ads GitHub prerelease uses the same com.majkeylab.scanit package as the Play app and includes Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing. These SDKs are not present in the stable Play or GitHub variants. ScanIt requests consent where required before initializing or loading ads and provides a Privacy options control in App info when Google requires it.

Google states that its current Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP address, product interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views, diagnostic information such as launch time, hangs, and energy use, and device or account identifiers including Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and encrypts it in transit using TLS. See Google's GMA Next-Gen data disclosure and Privacy Policy.

ScanIt does not intentionally pass scanned pages, OCR text, generated PDFs, saved images, email defaults, or selected recipients to the advertising SDK. Advertising begins only after a completed scan reaches Result. The stable scanning, cleanup, OCR, saving, and sharing code continues to process document content locally.

Premium purchases

Google Play Billing returns the Premium product identifier, purchase state, acknowledgement state, and purchase token needed to buy or restore Premium for the current Google Play account. ScanIt does not receive payment-card details, does not log purchase tokens, and does not persist a local Premium entitlement. Only a confirmed purchase of the matching product unlocks Document Actions and removes ads. A pending purchase grants nothing until Google Play confirms it.

Information ScanIt does not request

The stable Play and GitHub variants have no account system, advertising SDK, billing SDK, first-party analytics, app-owned Internet access, contact access, location access, or notification access. The VIP Ads beta adds Internet access plus Google advertising, consent, and Play Billing components to the Play-compatible package. No variant requests its own camera permission because the scanner uses the Google Play services camera flow, and no variant requests broad legacy storage access.

Support email and GitHub issues

If you email support or create a GitHub issue, the information you choose to submit is used to respond, investigate a report, and maintain the project. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Support correspondence is retained only while reasonably needed for support, security, or record-keeping, then deleted when no longer needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the support address, subject to legal or security retention needs.

This website

The ScanIt site contains no maintainer-added analytics, JavaScript, forms, advertising, tracking pixels, web fonts, or cookies. It loads its stylesheet and QR image from the same site.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security and may process other service data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. ScanIt adds no code that exposes those hosting logs to the maintainer.

External links, including GitHub, email, and Buy Me a Coffee, are opened only when you select them. Those services apply their own privacy terms. ScanIt does not receive payment-card or billing details from Buy Me a Coffee.

Children

ScanIt is intended for an adult document workflow and is not directed to children under 18. It has no account or social features.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before the public app materially changes its data handling. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or deletion requests about information sent directly to the maintainer can be emailed to majkeylab@gmail.com.
