# Privacy Policy

The canonical SeliaScan Privacy Policy is published at
<https://majkey25.github.io/ScanIt/privacy.html>. Its source is
[`docs/privacy.html`](docs/privacy.html).

The stable GitHub edition has no SeliaScan account, advertising SDK, Billing
SDK, first-party analytics, maintainer-operated document server, or
generative-AI client. The Google Play edition and the clearly marked Ads +
Premium GitHub prerelease use package `com.majkeylab.scanit` and include Google
Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing.

Ordinary scanning, OCR, barcode detection, redaction, cleanup, signing, PDF
creation, and image creation run on-device. Google Play services may download
ML Kit scanner or recognition modules before first use and may process the
limited diagnostic and usage telemetry described in
[Google's ML Kit disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).
SeliaScan does not send document pages, OCR text, saved files, signatures, or
email content to a maintainer-operated service or advertising SDK.

In the Ads + Premium edition, Google's advertising and consent SDKs may process
IP address, product interactions, diagnostics, Android advertising ID, app set
ID, and related device identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud
prevention. UMP requests consent where required and exposes Privacy options in
App info. Google states that transmitted SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Google Play Billing returns product identifiers, purchase state,
acknowledgement state, and purchase tokens needed to buy or restore monthly or
lifetime Premium for the current Google Play account. SeliaScan does not receive
payment-card details, does not log purchase tokens, and does not persist a local
Premium entitlement. A confirmed purchase removes ads and unlocks all features.

Temporary scan working copies are kept in a bounded app-private cache. Durable
PDFs and images are stored only according to the user's settings and actions.
SeliaScan deletes exact tracked outputs only after explicit Recent-screen
confirmation. Selecting a sharing app never deletes the saved copy. Android backup
and device transfer are disabled for app settings and private data.

Sharing, printing, email, external links, and selected storage providers receive
content only after a user action and apply their own privacy terms.

For privacy questions, email [majkeylab@gmail.com](mailto:majkeylab@gmail.com).
Do not send private documents or credentials.
